# App Store Review Notes

## Suggested promotional text

Monitor watchlists, rehearse strategies in simulation mode, and complete training and runbook workflows from one free iOS workspace.

## Suggested description

QuantumAIMobile is a free iOS workspace for watchlists, simulation, operational dashboards, and training guides.

Use the app to:

- monitor watchlists and local status panels
- rehearse rule-based strategies in simulation mode
- review queued local actions and workflow history
- inspect local reference and verification utilities on device
- complete training, onboarding, and runbook steps inside the app

Important:

- the App Store build is free of charge
- no purchase or paid subscription is required to access the visible feature set
- strategy actions shown in the app are used for local simulation and workflow review
- the default App Store flow does not route live orders or perform external checkout
- the app is not presented as an exchange, brokerage, or custodial service

## Suggested App Review notes

Hello App Review,

We revised the App Store metadata and visible UI to describe the App Store build more narrowly and accurately.

Clarifications for review:

1. This App Store build is free of charge. It does not require an in-app purchase, subscription purchase, or external cryptocurrency payment.
2. The App Store build is used for watchlists, simulation, training, operational runbooks, and local on-device utilities.
3. Strategy actions create local queued records for rehearsal and workflow review only. The default App Store flow does not route live orders to an exchange.
4. Reference, address, and verification screens are local utilities for display and on-device verification only. This App Store build does not store or transmit virtual currency on behalf of users.
5. The app is not presented as an exchange, brokerage, custodial wallet, or checkout product.

Suggested review path:

- Open `Panel` for the overview.
- Open `Botlar` and start a strategy to see local queue behavior.
- Open `Cüzdan` to verify local reference and verification utilities.
- Open `Ayarlar` and confirm the free access status.
